# Privacy Policy for Bonut

**Effective date:** August 3, 2026

Bonut ("**the App**", "**we**", "**us**") is operated by **Sharkship** ("**the Company**"). This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect from users of the Bonut mobile app, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

If you have questions about this policy or want to exercise any of the rights described below, contact us at **tech@sharkship.in**.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account information
- **Phone number** — used to create your account and log in via a one-time password (OTP). No separate password is stored.
- **Name** — provided during onboarding and shown to other users you transact with.

### 1.2 Identity verification (KYC)
To let you post loads or accept jobs as a verified user, we ask you to complete identity verification:
- **Aadhaar number and OTP** — submitted to a government-linked Aadhaar verification service to confirm your identity. We do not store your Aadhaar number in plain text after verification completes.
- **Selfie / face photo** — captured using your device camera. On-device face detection (Google ML Kit) checks that a face is properly framed before the photo is captured; the resulting photo is uploaded to our servers for manual/automated KYC approval and may be shown as your profile photo.

### 1.3 Vehicle and business information
- **Vehicle registration number** — verified against the government RTO/Vahan vehicle registry to confirm the vehicle exists and its details match what you enter.
- **Truck type, capacity, length, and height** — entered by you when adding a vehicle, used to match your truck to relevant loads.

### 1.4 Location information
- **Precise or approximate device location** — used to find your current location, and to search for and display nearby pickup/drop-off points and matching loads or trucks on a map. Location is only accessed while you are using the relevant feature (e.g. tapping "use current location" or viewing the truck map).

### 1.5 Contacts
- With your permission, we read your device's contact list to find other Bonut users among your existing contacts ("mutual connections") so you can see people you may already know on the platform. Your contacts are matched against our user base and are not shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

### 1.6 Photos and camera
- Camera access is used for KYC selfie capture. Photo library access lets you choose an existing photo (e.g. for your profile) instead of taking a new one.

### 1.7 Financial information
- **UPI ID** — collected when you request a payout ("redeem") from your in-app wallet, so we can send the payment to the correct account.
- **Wallet balance and transaction history** — including rewards earned (e.g. via scratch cards) and redemption requests.

### 1.8 Communications between users
- When you connect with another user to discuss a load or job, the App facilitates a call between the phone numbers on file. Basic call metadata (e.g. duration) may be recorded for support and safety purposes.

### 1.9 Device and usage information
- **Device identifiers, device model, OS version, and app version** — collected via standard device-info libraries to keep your account synced across devices and to help us debug issues.
- **Push notification token** — used to deliver notifications about your loads, jobs, rewards, and account status via Firebase Cloud Messaging. You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information above to:
- Create and secure your account, and verify your identity and vehicle so the marketplace stays trustworthy.
- Match you with relevant loads, trucks, or drivers based on location, truck type, and capacity.
- Process wallet rewards and redemption payouts.
- Send you push notifications about activity relevant to you.
- Show you mutual connections from your contacts, with your permission.
- Investigate misuse, fraud, or safety issues, and respond to support requests.
- Improve the App's reliability and features.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell your personal information.

## 3. How We Share Your Information

- **Other users** — your name, profile photo, verification status, vehicle details, and phone number (for calls) are visible to other users you interact with through search, matching, or job posting, as is standard for a two-sided logistics marketplace.
- **Government verification services** — your Aadhaar number and vehicle registration number are shared with the relevant government-linked verification APIs (Aadhaar eKYC, RTO/Vahan) solely to confirm your identity and vehicle.
- **Service providers** — we use infrastructure and processing partners (e.g. Google Firebase for push notifications and app infrastructure) who process data on our behalf under their own confidentiality and security obligations.
- **Payment rails** — UPI redemption requests are passed to payment processing partners to complete the transfer.
- **Legal requirements** — we may disclose information if required by law, regulation, or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Bonut, our users, or the public.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active and as needed to comply with legal, tax, or regulatory obligations (for example, KYC and financial transaction records may be retained longer where required by Indian law). When you request account deletion, we delete or anonymize your personal information except where retention is legally required.

## 5. Data Security

We use industry-standard measures — including encrypted storage for sensitive credentials on your device and encrypted transport (HTTPS) for data sent to our servers — to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 6. Your Rights and Choices

- **Access and correction** — you can view and update most of your profile information in the App.
- **Permissions** — you can grant or revoke camera, location, contacts, and notification permissions at any time in your device settings. Revoking a permission may limit related features (e.g. you won't be able to search by current location without location access).
- **Account and data deletion** — to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, email **tech@sharkship.in** with your registered phone number. We will process your request within a reasonable time, subject to any legally required retention.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Bonut is intended for commercial drivers, fleet owners, and shippers and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18 years of age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
